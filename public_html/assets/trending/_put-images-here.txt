Photos for the trending designs (/trending-designs/)

Each design has its own folder here, named after its key in includes/data/trends.php:
  /assets/trending/<design-key>/

Upload 7 to 10 photos per design (JPG, PNG or WebP, landscape 3:2, at least 1600 px wide).
Name them in plain words, in the order you want them shown:
  01-living-room-with-cane-sofa.jpg
  02-modular-kitchen-with-island.jpg  …
The file name becomes the alt text. As soon as a folder has photos they replace the
SAMPLE illustrations for that design, on the design page, its card and the home page.

Easiest: /admin/images.php → find "Trending design: …" → upload into slots 01 to 10.

samples/ holds the drawn SAMPLE illustrations. Delete it only after every design has real photos.
